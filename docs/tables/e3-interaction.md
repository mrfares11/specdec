Generated by `scripts/e3_interaction.py` from E2's per-prompt latency.
No new GPU work. All quantities are paired by prompt; intervals are
percentile bootstraps over prompts (400 resamples, seed 20260904).

`I` = G(scaled) - G(v1.0)  ---  does a better drafter get more from the tree?
`T` = G(EAGLE)  ---  does the tree transfer to a drafter we did not train?

## Permitted claims

- Interaction is positive with a 95% interval excluding zero in **1 of 4** cells.  A statistically supported positive interaction may be claimed for those cells only.
- Transfer to the official EAGLE drafter is positive with a 95% interval excluding zero in **4 of 4** cells.  Transfer may be claimed, scoped to cross-drafter transfer for the same verifier.

Percentages must be regenerated from unrounded latency, never from rounded speedups.
